Ring carries the tire's worst status; the two reading bands carry their own tint, so PSI and tread stay independently readable without a loud fill.
Number badge is fixed to the top-left corner and always matches the ring — the driver can call out "tire 8" to the shop.
Middle band is the photo/damage control: neutral square when empty, indigo when a photo is attached, red ! plus DMG when a photo is required and missing.
Spares drop the PSI band for a SPARE cap and keep tread only.
Em dash means no reading yet — never 0.
